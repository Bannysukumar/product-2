2. Features & Functionalities

2.1 User Registration & Login System
• Users register using OTP-based Phone Verification.
• Email ID field is optional at registration.
• Users can log in using either Phone Number or Email ID.
• Users set a password during registration, which will be the login password.
• After registration, users can optionally update their Email ID:
o Email verification via OTP sent to email.
o After verification, Email ID can be used as a login ID.
• Users who do not enter a Referral ID are automatically mapped under Admin.
• Admin can later manually reassign these free or paid members under any Paid Member.

2.2 Paid Membership & Payment System
• Users pay a one-time membership fee of ₹3,200 to become a Paid Member.
• Payment Gateway: Secure online payment via integrated merchant gateway.
• Successful payment activates:
o Generation of a Unique Member ID (also acts as Referral ID).
o Access to exclusive discounts and referral eligibility.
• Users receive a Shopping Card displaying:
o Member ID / Referral ID
o Full Name
o Phone Number
• The Shopping Card is downloadable and can be shown at partnered stores for discounts.

2.3 Referral System & Earnings (Backend Managed Only)
• Each Paid Member receives a unique Referral Link & Referral ID.
• Unlimited direct referrals allowed.
• Referral Tree Visibility:
o Direct Referrals (Level 1): All referred users' names visible.
o Level 2 onwards: Only total referral numbers per level displayed.
• Referral Bonus structure (calculated in backend, hidden from users):
Level Referral Bonus %
Level 1 5%
Level 2 3%
Level 3 2%
Level 4 1%
Level 5 1%
• Referral Bonus calculations handled in backend only and not visible publicly.

2.4 Earnings & Withdrawal System
• Users track total earnings (Referral Bonuses) in real-time via dashboard.
• Withdrawal requests allowed only after earnings reach a minimum of ₹500.
• Withdrawal Methods Available:
o Bank Transfer
o PhonePe
• Manual Admin Approval & Processing (Within 24 to 48 hours):
o Admin enters amount processed and selects payment method used:
▪ Bank Transfer
▪ PhonePe
▪ Google Pay
▪ Paid in Cash
o Admin marks withdrawal as "Payment Processed".
o Processed amount deducted from user's total available balance.
o Updated balance shown clearly to users.
o Transaction recorded in transaction history for both Admin and User.

2.5 Membership Benefits & Discounts
• Exclusive discounts at partnered shops across various categories:
o Gold Jewelry
o Groceries
o Clothing & Apparel
o Home Appliances
• Members use their Shopping Card at partnered stores to redeem discounts.
• Homepage showcases these benefits through an engaging carousel/grid view.
• Clear CTA: "See All Membership Benefits," linking to a dedicated detailed page.

2.6 Business Owners & Vendor Partnership Section
• A dedicated section and page targeting Shop Owners, Business Owners, Vendors.
• Clearly explains the benefits of partnering with Vehash Global Marketing:
o Increased footfall by referrals of Vehash Global's Paid Members.
o More customer visibility & increased business sales.
• Easy form submission for vendors interested in partnering.

2.7 Website Navigation & User Experience
• Public Pages (Before Login):
o Home Page: Introduction, key benefits, CTA for joining.
o How It Works: Clear guide on registration, membership, & referral mechanism.
o Membership Benefits: Details of discounts & categories.
o Vendor Partnership (Dedicated section/page).
o Contact Us (For user support and inquiries).
• Fully responsive design (Mobile optimized).
• Sticky Navigation Bar ensuring easy access to key pages & actions.

2.8 Payment & Transaction History
• User Dashboard:
o Clear view of available balance.
o Detailed record of past withdrawals and transaction history.
o Withdrawal request statuses (Pending, Processed, Rejected).
• Admin Dashboard:
o Comprehensive record of all processed withdrawals.
o Detailed transactions (Amount, Date, Payment Method, User details).
o Ability to filter transactions by user, date, payment status, and method.

2.9 Admin Dashboard (For Platform Management)
• User Management:
o View, approve, suspend, or delete registered Free & Paid Members.
• Referral Management:
o View referral hierarchy and total referral numbers at each level.
o Manually reassign Free or Paid Users to different Paid Members.
• Withdrawal Management:
o View, approve/reject withdrawal requests.
o Enter withdrawal amounts, select payment method, and mark "Payment Processed".
• Comprehensive reporting: Referral bonuses, earnings, and transaction history.

3. Technical Architecture & System Requirements

3.1 Technology Stack
• Frontend: Next.js (React Framework)
• Styling: Tailwind CSS
• Backend: Next.js API Routes (Server-side Logic)
• Database: MongoDB
• Authentication: Next/Auth (Secure authentication)
• Payment Gateway: Integrated Merchant Gateway (Razorpay, PayPal, Stripe)
• Hosting: Hostinger KVM4
• SSL Encryption: Enabled (HTTPS)
